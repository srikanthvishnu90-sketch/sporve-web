2. Embedded AI Operations:
   AI actions (session recaps, parent reminders, rebooking nudges) are integrated
   directly into coach workflows with mandatory human-in-the-loop approval, saving
   coaches hours every week.

3. Instant 1-Click Booking:
   Frictionless booking checkout (under 90 seconds cold / under 10 seconds rebook)
   with Apple Pay and transparent pricing with zero junk fee stacking.

4. Continuous Compliance as a Product:
   Background check and coach verification state fails closed with continuous
   webhook updates, replacing one-off stale badges.

5. Team & Open Capacity Discovery:
   Syndicates open camp, clinic, and team spots directly into public search—a
   massive white space ignored by TeamSnap and SportsEngine.

PAYMENT SECURITY CONTROLS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. No Client Price Tampering:
   Booking creation only sends IDs (searcher_id, session_id, athlete_id). Server trigger
   trg_set_booking_price reads base price and tier multipliers to calculate total.

2. Snapshot Policy Refund Engine:
   Cancellation refunds follow the policy snapshotted at time of purchase (flexible,
   moderate, strict), calculated strictly by server functions.

3. Stripe Connect Onboarding:
   Coaches connect bank accounts directly via Stripe Express. Sporve never stores raw
   bank details or sensitive credentials.
