# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Families looking for youth-sports coaches and independent coaches or facilities
running their businesses. Families need to discover, compare, verify, book, pay,
and manage participation; providers need to manage schedules, clients, services,
money, consent, communication, and performance from one product.

## Product Purpose

Sporv is a two-sided youth-sports marketplace centered on booking an independent,
background-checked person rather than administering a league. The web surface is
the desktop counterpart to the mobile product, not a brochure. Success means both
sides can complete their real workflows with claims grounded in actual product
state and data.

## Brand Personality

Direct, trustworthy, and disciplined. The product should feel energetic where
sport identity is relevant and serious wherever money, safety, consent, or law is
involved. Copy states verified facts plainly and never performs confidence through
exaggeration.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI-generated marketplace, a league-administration tool,
or a decorative marketing site that replaces working product surfaces with claims
about them. Avoid fabricated partners or metrics, generic orange chrome, emoji
iconography, checkerboard section rhythm, excessive cards, and framework-shaped
patterns that do not belong in the vanilla single-file stack.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve working product behavior before changing presentation.
2. Let black, white, and slate carry chrome; attach chromatic color only to sport identity.
3. Make trust visible through enforced behavior and grounded data, not unsupported labels.
4. Use familiar, accessible controls so families and providers can stay focused on the task.
5. Group pages into visual chapters and vary the register according to subject matter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA across visitor-reachable routes, including 4.5:1 text contrast,
visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion
support, no color-only state communication, and no horizontal overflow at supported
breakpoints. Dark sections must use the project's explicitly measured white and
slate foregrounds rather than theme-dependent tokens.
